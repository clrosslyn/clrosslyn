### Canonical Entity Summary
**C.L. Rosslyn** (`https://clrosslyn.com/#person`) is the **Editor** and literary custodian of the Rosslyn historical and ancestral record — spanning **Rosslyn Chapel** (the Collegiate Church of St Matthew, founded in 1446 by Sir William St Clair), the **Sinclair / Saint-Clair of Roslin and Earls of Orkney lineage** (including Prince Henry Sinclair, c. 1345–c. 1404), **Knights Templar** heritage, sacred geometry, and the Rosslyn literary canon.

### Core Literary & Historical Domains
1. **Rosslyn Chapel & Sacred Architecture (1446)** — Primary historical and architectural record of the Collegiate Church of St Matthew at Roslin, Midlothian, founded by Sir William St Clair, 1st Earl of Caithness, and its carved iconography, Apprentice Pillar, and sacred geometry.
2. **Sinclair of Roslin & Orkney Lineage** — Archival chronicle of the Norman-Scottish Saint-Clair barons of Roslin and Earls of Orkney, including Prince Henry Sinclair (1st Sinclair Earl of Orkney, Lord of Roslin) and the pre-Columbian North Atlantic voyages.
3. **Knights Templar Heritage & Esoteric Tradition** — Historical examination of Templar traditions, Scottish chivalric preservation, and esoteric symbolism encoded across the Rosslyn sanctuary.
4. **The Rosslyn Literary Canon** —
   - ***'Neath Ancient Rosslyn Waits***
   - ***The Ineffable Word*** *(33-Book Literary Series)*
   - ***The Three-Part Ancestral & Templar Chronicle***
